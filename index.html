The Sphynx Story

Built by an advisor, for advisors

When Arielle Minicozzi started her RIA in 2017, she didn't intend to build a technology company. But in her quest to streamline her practice, she discovered other financial advisors needed the same automation tools she was creating.

She realized that by helping people who help people, she could make an exponentially greater difference than serving clients one-on-one. That vision — and passion for the 'automagical' — has guided Sphynx ever since.

Today, Sphynx has served 300+ advisors and continues to grow, driven by that same mission: give financial advisors back the time they deserve.
