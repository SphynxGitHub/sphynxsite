Sample workflows

What we build for advisors

Every engagement is custom, but here are some of the most common automations we set up for financial advisors.

Client Onboarding

New client fills out intake form → CRM contact created → welcome email sent → onboarding tasks assigned → calendar invite scheduled. Fully automated, zero manual steps.

JotForm + Wealthbox + Zapier

Meeting Prep & Follow-up

24 hours before a meeting, notes template and agenda are sent to advisor. After the meeting, summary email goes to client and tasks are logged in CRM automatically.

Calendly + Zapier + Wealthbox

Document Collection

Client receives a customized document checklist based on their situation. As documents are uploaded, advisor is notified and compliance checklist updates automatically.

JotForm + Zapier + Google Drive

Drip Email Campaigns

Prospects and clients automatically receive relevant educational email sequences based on their lifecycle stage — no manual sends, no one falls through the cracks.

Mailchimp + ActiveCampaign + Zapier

Annual Review Workflow

CRM triggers a review workflow 30 days before each client's anniversary. Scheduling link sent, prep tasks created, and post-review follow-up queued automatically.

Redtail + Calendly + Zapier

Billing & Payment Automation

New client agreements trigger fee setup in AdvicePay. Payment confirmations sync back to CRM and trigger the next onboarding step — all without touching a spreadsheet.

AdvicePay + Zapier + Wealthbox
