What's Included

Everything in every plan

Weekly Error Monitoring

Sphynx signs up to receive copies of your Zapier error notifications. We review them every week, identify issues, and prioritize what needs to be fixed — so you never have a broken automation running silently in the background.

Maintenance & Update Hours

Your allocated hours cover both hands-on maintenance work and check-in meetings. Use them when apps update and break integrations, when you need to add new automations, or whenever your workflows need a tune-up.

Meeting Time Included

Strategy and check-in meetings are drawn from your allocated hours — so you're never paying extra just to talk through what needs to be done. We keep it efficient and focused.

Flexible Overage Hours

If you exceed your plan's allotted hours, you can purchase additional time at your tier's discounted rate — or upgrade to a higher tier at any time. You're never stuck or left without support.

Plan Terms

The fine print, made readable

Plan Term

Plans run for one year from the date the contract is signed, paid on a monthly basis. Unused hours at the end of the year do not carry forward.

Upgrades & Downgrades

  • Upgrades to a higher tier can happen at any time
  • Downgrades require 30 days written notice
  • Downgrades are only allowed if you haven't exceeded the new tier's hours

Cancellation by Client

30 days written notice required. Any unpaid hours up to the plan's allotment are billed at the tier's hourly rate. Excess hours are billed at $300/hr. Fees for unused hours are non-refundable.

Cancellation by Sphynx

30 days written notice. Any unpaid hours are billed at the tier's hourly rate. Fees already paid for unused hours will be refunded.
