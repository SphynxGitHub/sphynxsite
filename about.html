FAQ

Common questions

What does Sphynx Automation do?
Our team provides technology consulting and custom automation solutions to help financial advisors save time, make more money, and lead more balanced lives. For an overview, check out this webinar.
What products and services do you offer?
We offer three types of products: customized automation services, hourly technology coaching, and DIY automation courses & templates.
What's your process for automation projects?
Most engagements take about 4–6 weeks from when pre-work is completed, depending on the scope and our workload. Schedule an intro call and we'll walk you through everything.
How much do your services cost?
Coaching is charged by the hour (paid at booking). DIY courses and templates are purchased individually. Automation services are a-la-carte and fully customized — schedule a call to receive a custom proposal. We accept payments via Stripe.
Do you provide process reviews?
We add the most value in the automation technology arena, so while some process review is involved in each project, it's not our primary focus. We're happy to refer you to our trusted Virtual COO and Virtual Assisting partners for deeper process work.
