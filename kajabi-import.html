Import Lessons from Kajabi

Use the bookmarklet to scrape lesson content from your Kajabi course pages, then import it directly into your Sphynx store products.

1 Install the scraper bookmarklet

Drag this button to your browser bookmarks bar. You'll click it while viewing a Kajabi lesson to extract the content.

Sphynx: Scrape Lesson
Drag this button to your bookmarks bar

The bookmarklet is completely safe — it only reads content from the current page and copies it to your clipboard. It never sends data anywhere automatically.

2 Scrape your Kajabi lessons

1. Log into Kajabi and open a course lesson page
2. Click the Sphynx: Scrape Lesson bookmark
3. The lesson data will be copied to your clipboard
4. Come back here and paste it in the box below
5. Repeat for each lesson

Tip: You can paste multiple lessons one after another — just keep pasting and clicking Parse.


3 Review & assign to a product

Check the extracted lessons below, edit any content if needed, then assign them to a product and import.
